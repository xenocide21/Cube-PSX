Cube PSX - credits
==================

Cube PSX: the PlayStation port of Cube, by Xenocide21 (2026). Programmed with Claude (Anthropic).

Cube, the game and engine it is ported from: Wouter van Oortmerssen (Aardappel), 2001-2005.
  http://cubeengine.com  -  thank you, Wouter, for Cube, and for welcoming the port.

Everything below comes from the Cube release (2005), converted for the PlayStation, except where it says otherwise.
The authors' own readmes are in the licenses/ folder. Free, non-commercial distribution only: see LICENSE.txt.
