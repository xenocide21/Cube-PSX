==================

three kinds of terms:

1. The program (the source code in src/, tools/, test/ and rig/) - the Cube license (zlib, with one extra clause)
2. The game media (maps, textures, models, sounds, music) - each piece keeps its author's own terms (see CREDITS.txt
   and the licenses/ folder): together, they allow free, non-commercial distribution only
3. New work made for Cube PSX (the XENO maps, Facing Cubes, the portraits and voices) - see the end of this file

2. The game media
-----------------

Cube's own license covers only its source code: "Game media included in the cube game (maps, textures, sounds, models
etc.) are not covered by this license, and may have individual copyrights and distribution restrictions (see
individual readmes)." Cube PSX carries the same media, converted for the PlayStation (smaller textures, fewer colours,
fewer polygons, compressed sound), so the same restrictions apply. The ones that matter most:

  - NON-COMMERCIAL ONLY. Cube PSX may not be sold, nor bundled with anything sold, nor paid for in any way. Several
    authors forbid profit (the ikbase and ik2k textures, the Max Payne textures by Remedy, the Bauul and Ratamahatta
    models, the Piglet and WSG maps).
  - BY DOWNLOAD. The ikbase and ik2k textures (used on most maps) may be distributed by electronic media only: share
    the download; don't press or sell discs. Burning your own copy to play is fine.
  - CREDIT. Every author is credited in CREDITS.txt, which must travel with the game; their original readmes are in
    licenses/ and travel with it too.

Some media is under the GNU GPL v2: the armour and invulnerability models (Seth and Ulrich Galbraith). Their original
files, the preferred form for changing them, are in licenses/gpl-models/; tools/mkmodel.py converts them.

Each author's own words are in licenses/: where this summary and an author's readme differ, the readme wins.
