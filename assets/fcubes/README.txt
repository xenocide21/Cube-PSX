Facing Cubes - a Cube (2005) take on Unreal Tournament's Facing Worlds, made for Cube PSX.
To play it in Cube on PC: copy fcubes.cgz and fcubes.cfg into Cube's packages/base folder, then in Cube
open the console (~) and type:  /map fcubes
(Cube itself has no CTF: the flag bases are entities Cube ignores. The space sky is Cube PSX's own.)

Author: Xenocide21 (2026). Free to play, share and use as a base for your own maps, non-commercially, with credit to
Xenocide21. Textures: egyptsoc by Sock (from the Cube release; credit Sock in your readme if you use them).
