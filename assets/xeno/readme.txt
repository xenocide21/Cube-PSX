------------------------ XENO - a map pack for Cube ------------------------
Author:   Xenocide21
Made:     2026, for Cube PSX (the PlayStation port of Cube); these are the same maps for Cube on the PC
Maps:     xeno01-xeno08 (single player campaign: sp xeno01 ... each exit leads to the next)
          xdm1, xdm2, xdm3 (multiplayer: deathmatch; their CTF flags and hill are Cube PSX's - Cube ignores them)
Textures: from the Cube release - tech1soc and egyptsoc by Sock, ikbase and ik2k by Iikka "Fingers" Keranen,
          golgotha by Crack dot Com (public domain)
Install:  put the files in cube/packages/base/ (or a folder of your own, and play them from there)

Permissions:
You're free to play, share and use these maps as a base for your own, non-commercially, with credit to Xenocide21.
The textures keep their authors' terms (non-commercial; see the readmes that come with Cube).
